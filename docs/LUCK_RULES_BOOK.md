# Luck by Chance rules book (plain words), owner-approved redesign, 31 Oct 2026

This replaces the survivors / re-throw idea. The live site is unchanged until the site adapter is switched on behind its flag. Each round's target number, and each player's fate number, are made by the Luck site from the game id and the public beacon, and given to the module.

## The game
1. A game has 22 rounds (22 dice rounds), fixed; the Organizer cannot change it. Every player plays all 22. Nobody is eliminated.
2. Each round the press window is 10 seconds. It is the only choice. Between rounds there is a 10-second gap; the 3-second reveal happens inside that gap.
3. Each round shows a target number from 1 to 6. A player presses to stop their dice on a number.

## Length: about 10 minutes in all
The waiting room opens 120 s (2 minutes) before the published start; people join and wait, and the list closes at the start. Each round is a 10 s press window plus a 10 s gap = 20 s. 22 rounds x 20 s = 440 s = 7 min 20 s of play (the last gap is not waited for: 7 min 10 s start to last result). Play time must be at least 7:00 and at most 8:00; the whole game from waiting-room open to final result is at most 10:00 (120 s + 440 s = 9 min 20 s, then the results screen). A pause adds its own time.

## Scoring (per round, max 220 over the game)
4. Exact = 10 points. 1 away = 5. 2 away = 3. 3 or more away = 1. No press in time = 0.

## Ranking (never tied)
5. Final ranking by total points. Ties: most exact hits, then most 5-point hits, then the player who reached that total first (earlier round), then the lower fate number. A prize is never shared.

## Prizes and rules
6. The top five overall win 1st to 5th. The button says "You won"; there is no Claim button. The Organizer writes the prize text for each place.
7. At least 2 players are needed. The player list closes when the game starts. Pause freezes every clock (Organizer or Host only). On the new flow the cap is 2000 players; the live path keeps its 100,000 packs. The Organizer's only settings are the prize text and the start mode (auto or manual).

## Reactions (shared Reaction pack)
8. Exact hit: "Boom!". 1 away: "Nice one!". Miss or no press: "Oops!". Round start: "Round starts!". Countdown: "3 2 1". Final result: "Ta-da!".
